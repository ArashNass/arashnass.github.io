    Controls & combat

    Moving

    ← → run · ↑ / Space jump, and jump again in the air for a super jump. Land on a foe from above for a double blow. On a phone, use the buttons at the bottom of the screen.

    Fighting

    X strike with the ox-head mace. Three quick strikes end in a hard finisher. Keep holding Strike after a swing until the mace glows, then let go for a heavy blow that interrupts ordinary foes. Shift / C dodges: no blow can touch you mid-dash. Dodge just as a blow arrives to earn Farr and a stronger counterstrike. From the fifth labour, when Olad is bound, K / L throws Rostam’s lasso: it drags a foe to your feet, reeling, where your next blow lands harder; it holds a dazed boss a moment longer, hauls you up to the golden rings, pulls a flier from the sky, and draws in provisions out of reach.

    Bosses

    Each great foe has an opening. When stars circle its head it is dazed: strike now, and the boss bar flashes Strike now! Otherwise your blows glance off. Watch for the warning glow before its big attacks; the corner ledge is a refuge, but you must come down to win.

    Hearts and fires

    Roast lamb and glowing hearts restore you; in boss fights a heart appears in a corner now and then. Walk past a fire altar to light it: if you fall, you rise there. Some fires draw a stand: hold the clearing through three waves for a feast. Foes with a golden glow are champions, tougher and richer. At a lit fire press ↓ (or tap Offer) for the Fire Temple.

    Provisions and Farr

    Provisions fill your purse, to spend at the Fire Temple on lasting gifts, and your golden Farr: when it is full press F and the Simurgh strikes every foe on screen. Gather eight in a labour and Rakhsh gallops in to kick the boss.

    Simurgh feathers

    Three feathers glow on the highest perches of every labour. The map shows how many you have found. Find all twenty-one for the Simurgh's blessing: one more heart, for ever.

    Simurgh trials

    Golden feathers marked Simurgh trial offer optional skill routes. Stand beside one and press Down, or tap Trial. Gather three golden sparks before time runs out without taking a hit. Your first clear earns three provisions and Farr; later clears can improve your best time. You can always continue the main journey.

    Gamepad

    Stick or D-pad move · A jump · X strike (hold for heavy) · B dodge · Y Farr · Start menu. In menus the D-pad moves, A presses and B goes back.

    Your way

    Choose the Gentle road on the home screen for more hearts and gentler bosses. In Settings set the volumes, turn off screen shake or flashes, and change any key.
