Structural & earthquake engineering tools

I built these free, interactive tools to make earthquake and structural engineering concepts easier to understand and use, whether you're a student learning the fundamentals, an engineer checking a design, or a researcher exploring seismic risk. Everything runs directly in your browser, no downloads or sign ups needed.
