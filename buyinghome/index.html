Rates are UK market averages from Moneyfacts, 5 October 2026 (2-year fixed 5.98%, 5-year fixed 6.00%, 2-year tracker 4.55%, SVR 7.13%; Bank Rate 3.75%), adjusted for loan to value. Your actual rate depends on lender and credit profile. Term is capped so the mortgage ends by age 70, max 35 years. Take-home uses 2026/27 England income tax and employee National Insurance, with pension deducted before tax (net pay); student loans are not included. Stamp duty uses current England and Northern Ireland rates, including the 5% second home and 2% non-UK resident surcharges; Scotland and Wales have their own taxes. A guide only, not financial advice.
